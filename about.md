# Auto Deco

Finished the layout but not sure how to decorate the level?

**Auto Deco** adds an editor button that themes your entire level in one click. Pick a theme —
Hell, Futuristic, Neon, Heaven, Void, Retro, Toxic or Sunset — and the mod writes in the
background colours, a repeating background pulse, a screen shake where it fits, and a swaying
move trigger for whatever decoration you had selected.

Nothing is drawn over your level: every effect is a normal Geometry Dash trigger, so it saves
with the level and works for anyone who plays it, with or without the mod.

- Eight themes, all defined in one file so they are easy to tweak or extend
- Settings for colour fade-in, pulse density, shake and drift distance
- Undo with `Ctrl+Z`, or remove the theme from the same panel
- Works in the editor only, and never touches your objects except to group the selection you
  chose to drift
